ABOUT FAIRMONEY DEBIT CARD

What is Fairmoney
FairMoney is a digital bank licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN). You can open an account easily from the app in just a few minutes, with no branch visit needed. FairMoney offers loans from ₦1,500 to ₦10,000,000 for a period of one to twelve months. The more you use FairMoney bank, the better your loans!
How can i create a Fairmoney bank account
Download the FairMoney app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store, provide your phone number, verify with OTP, complete your profile with BVN and ID, and your account is ready to use. The entire process takes just a few minutes.
What makes Fairmoney loanss better
FairMoney offers instant approval within 5 minutes, no collateral required, flexible repayment terms, and competitive interest rates. Our digital-first approach means no paperwork or branch visits needed.

CREATING AN ACCOUNT

How can I create a FairMoney bank account?
Download the FairMoney app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store, sign up with your phone number, verify with OTP, provide your BVN for verification, and your account is ready in minutes.
How much do you charge for my FairMoney bank account?
FairMoney accounts have no monthly maintenance fees, no minimum balance requirements, and no hidden charges. You only pay for services you actively use like transfers to other banks.

BORROWING

How much can I borrow and for how long?
You can borrow from ₦1,500 up to ₦10,000,000 depending on your credit profile and loan type. Repayment periods range from 61 days to 18 months for personal loans, and up to 36 months for asset finance.
Do I need documents or a collateral?
No, FairMoney loans are unsecured. You don't need any collateral or guarantor to access our loans. You only need your BVN for verification - no physical documents or paperwork required.
